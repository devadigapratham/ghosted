Ghosted

Logs every job you apply to on Handshake, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, LinkedIn and more, so you know exactly who ghosted you.

Get it on GitHub Try the live demo

What it does

  • Logs applications automatically. Apply to something and a confirmation box appears pre-filled. Press Enter and it's recorded.
  • Flags sponsorship before you apply. It reads the job description and badges the page: sponsors, no sponsorship, citizens/PR only, or unclear — with the exact sentence it matched, so you can judge yourself.
  • Chases follow-ups. Every application gets a follow-up date, and a daily check tells you what's gone quiet.
  • Shows the damage. How many applied, how many interviews, how many ghosted, and what share ever replied.
  • Works where you actually apply. Handshake, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Workable, LinkedIn and Indeed, plus one-click logging on any other careers page. A Source column records which.

Where the data lives

On your machine. There's no server, no account, and no signup, so there's nothing to breach and nothing to bill you for. Connect a Google Sheet if you want a copy you can share or open on your phone — it's your sheet, in your Google account.

Try it without installing

The live demo is the real dashboard running on sample data. Click around: filter the table, move something along the pipeline, flip the theme. Nothing is saved and none of it is anyone's real data.

The same dashboard without ?demo=1 reads a copy kept in your own browser, so you can export a CSV from the extension and import it here to read your data on a machine that has no extension.

Capture, sponsorship scanning, reminders and Sheets sync need the extension — they have to run inside the browser on the job page itself. The hosted version is for reading and editing what you already collected.

Install

No terminal, no Node, no npm. About a minute.

  1. On the repo, click the green Code button → Download ZIP.
  2. Unzip it and move the folder somewhere permanent. Chrome loads the extension from that folder, so don't delete it afterwards.
  3. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the folder.
  5. Apply to a job. That's it.

Google Sheets sync is optional and needs a one-time setup; the README walks through it. Everything else works immediately.
